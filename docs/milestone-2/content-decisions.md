# Milestone 2 — Content decisions

`BRIEFING.md` (root, English, base language) is the single source of truth for the public site's content, and `docs/milestone-2/BRIEFING.es.md` is the official Spanish version used for the `es/` pages. Both are the official 4Geeks files (repo `4GeeksAcademy/ai-engineering-syllabus`, commit `ae89516`), kept unmodified and checked by SHA-256 (see `verification.md`, S01).

These are the only places where the site deliberately differs from the briefing text:

| Briefing text | What the site does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "12 years of experience in the Latin American market" / "12 años de experiencia en el mercado latinoamericano" | "12 years of experience in the Spanish and US markets" / "12 años de experiencia en los mercados español y estadounidense" | Nexova operates from Valencia and Miami, as the briefing itself says ("Regional presence: Spain and United States"). Developer decision; `BRIEFING.md` is not changed. |
| Schema.org JSON: `"@context": "<https://schema.org>"` and `"url": "<https://nexova.com>"` (English briefing) | The page markup uses the plain URLs `https://schema.org` and `https://nexova.com` | The angle brackets are a Markdown autolink artefact and would make the JSON-LD invalid. Everything else is copied exactly, including `"addressRegion": "Comunidad Valenciana"`. The Spanish pages use the Spanish briefing's JSON (`"description": "Consultora de recursos humanos y adquisición de talento"`). |
| Emails in angle brackets: `<contacto@nexova.com>`, `<name@company.com>` | The site shows the plain address | Markdown autolinks, not part of the address. |
| Form option labels | Spanish labels come from the Spanish briefing; option `value` attributes are the same in both languages | One set of values for validation and a future backend, two sets of visible labels. |
| Language scope ("multilingual support is optional but highly recommended") | Bilingual site: English (base) at the root, Spanish under `es/` | Nexova operates between Spain and Miami; English stays complete as the base language. |
| Images (not part of the briefing text) | The landing page shows the two office photos `assets/valencia.webp` and `assets/miami.webp` (from the Milestone 1 design) | Images: office photos used with the developer's confirmed permission (2026-10-23), including the people visible in the Miami photo. |
| Landing sections ("Header, Hero, Services, Why Nexova, Contact, Footer, in this order") | The briefing sections keep that order and their exact texts. Three extra sections from the Milestone 1 landing sit between them: a marquee after the hero, "Process" after Services, and "Offices" after Why Nexova | Deliberate additions (S07, developer's decision): the brief asks for "at least two additional sections", and the site should look and feel like the Milestone 1 landing. Their texts come from M1 `content/home.ts`, limited to briefing facts (founded 2011, headquarters in Valencia, expansion office in Miami, about 120 employees, the three business lines and sectors); no invented figures, clients or testimonials. |
| Marquee words | Executive headhunting, Customer support outsourcing, Corporate training, Technology, Retail, Financial services, Valencia, Miami (ES: the same words from the Spanish briefing) | Only words from the briefing; decorative and hidden from screen readers. |
| Process (M1 `processSection`) | Four steps: Briefing, Screening, Interviews, Placement (ES: Briefing, Cribado, Entrevistas, Incorporación). Step 2 drops M1's "AI-assisted screening" | The briefing does not describe AI in Nexova's process. |
| "Sector specialization in technology, retail, and finance" | The sentence stays verbatim; the three sectors are also shown as large decorative words (Technology / Retail / Finance), without M1's industry descriptions | M1's industry descriptions add claims the briefing does not make. |
| M1 "Human & AI" section and hero role ticker | Not ported | Their claims are not in the briefing, and the hero text must be the briefing headline. |
| Hero illustration (not part of the briefing text) | Its visually hidden description says the animation shows "a pile of 72 CVs" | The number describes the decorative CV cascade (72 cards, as in Milestone 1), not a company figure. |
| "Why Nexova (2 columns)" | The four points sit 2 × 2 from `md:` (one column on phones), with the large Technology / Retail / Finance words as a full-width decorative row below | The briefing's two-column layout; S07 had briefly shown three figures side by side (fixed in S11 after the final audit). |
| Form field order (the briefing's table lists Availability before LinkedIn) | The LinkedIn field sits in the "Professional profile" group, before Availability | The briefing lists the fields but does not fix their order on screen; LinkedIn belongs with the professional details. All 11 fields, their types and their rules are unchanged (final audit, S13). |
